# Alpha Flow

**Control your flow. Build your future.**

Alpha Flow is a modern personal money flow, budgeting, and savings management application for Android built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Room Database.

## Features

- **Total Flow Balance & Waterfall Visualization**: See your financial waterfall (Income → Expenses → Savings → Remaining balance) with live percentage bars and flow indicators.
- **Account Management**: Support for multiple accounts (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money, Savings) with real-time balance calculations based on income, expenses, and inter-account transfers.
- **Income vs Expenses Analytics**: Interactive 6-month historical bar chart comparing monthly income against expenditures, complete with savings rate tracking.
- **Transactions Management**:
  - Record Income, Expenses, and Account Transfers with ease.
  - Search transactions and filter by transaction type, category, account, and date (Today, This month, All).
  - Modify or delete transactions with real-time balance integrity checks.
- **Savings Goals**:
  - Track target amounts, target dates, and percentage progress for every goal.
  - Seamlessly deposit funds into goals or withdraw funds back to accounts.
- **Smart Budgets & Category Spending**:
  - Monthly budget tracking per category with warning states (approaching limit at 80%, exceeded alerts at 100%+).
  - Breakdown of expenses by category with top-spending highlight stars.
- **Flow Insights & Health**:
  - Automated smart financial insights (transport spending shifts, savings rate improvements, budget alerts, unrecorded daily expenses).
  - Health metrics: Savings rate, Expense ratio, Budget usage %, Monthly net flow.
- **Close Your Day**: Daily closing checklist to confirm you've tracked all your expenses today.
- **Privacy & Security**:
  - Balance privacy toggle (`Hide`/`Show` balances).
  - 4-digit PIN lock screen protection with numeric keypad.
  - 100% on-device local storage via Room SQLite.
- **Data Portability**:
  - Full JSON export and import compatible with the Alpha Flow specification.
  - Start fresh or restore demo data at any time.
- **Adaptive Design & Theming**:
  - Material Design 3 theming supporting System, Light, and Dark modes.
  - Custom adaptive launcher icon with brand flow insignia.
